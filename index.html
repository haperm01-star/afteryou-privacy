Privacy Policy — AfterYou

Last updated: 18 November 2026

AfterYou is a single-player story game. It has no accounts, no sign-in, no social features and no advertising. This policy describes everything the app does with data, which is very little.

The short version

Your save game lives on your phone and nowhere else. We do not collect it, we cannot read it, and it is not sent anywhere. We have no servers of our own and no way to identify you.

What stays on your device

Everything you do in AfterYou is stored locally, in the app's own storage:

  • your progress through lives, realms and scenarios
  • your statistics, ranks and relationships with characters
  • achievements and reincarnations you have unlocked
  • anything you type into the in-game Notebook
  • which secrets and locks you have opened
  • app settings such as sound and haptics

None of this leaves your phone. It is not transmitted, backed up to our systems, or shared with anyone. We never see it.

If you uninstall AfterYou, this data is deleted with the app. The app is configured with Android backup disabled (allowBackup: false), which means your save is not copied into Google's device backup — so reinstalling or moving to a new phone starts a fresh game.

What leaves your device

### Over-the-air updates

AfterYou uses Expo Updates to deliver bug fixes and content updates without a new store download. When the app starts, it contacts Expo's update service (u.expo.dev) to ask whether a newer version exists.

That request necessarily includes technical information: your IP address, the app version and runtime version, and your device platform. It does not include your save game, your Notebook, or anything you typed.

Expo is operated by Expo (650 Industries, Inc.). Their privacy policy: https://expo.dev/privacy

### That is the complete list

AfterYou makes no other network connections. There is no analytics SDK, no crash reporter, no advertising SDK, no social login and no third-party tracker of any kind in the app.

Notifications

AfterYou can send you reminders — for example, that a life is waiting. These are local notifications: they are scheduled by the app on your own device and generated there. No notification is sent from a server, and the app does not register for or transmit a push token.

Android will ask your permission before any notification is shown. You can refuse, and you can revoke it later in Android Settings → Apps → AfterYou → Notifications. The game is fully playable with notifications switched off.

Permissions

PermissionWhy
POST_NOTIFICATIONSTo show the local reminders described above. Optional — decline it and nothing else changes

The app explicitly blocks the microphone permission (RECORD_AUDIO), which some audio libraries request by default. AfterYou plays sound; it never records any.

Children

AfterYou is not directed at children. It contains comic depictions of death, the afterlife, Heaven and Hell, and moral choices with consequences. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, of any age, because we do not collect personal information at all.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to export, correct or delete on your behalf. You can delete everything the app has stored by uninstalling it, or by using Android Settings → Apps → AfterYou → Storage → Clear data, which resets the game to a fresh start.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of AfterYou adds anything that collects or transmits data, this policy will be updated before that version is released, and the date at the top will change.
